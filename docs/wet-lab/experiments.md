guide:
  title: How to Use / Read
  subtitle: A quick guide to finding experiments and understanding each entry.
  opening: This library brings together the experimental procedures used throughout
    ORCA. Browse by category, search for a specific method, and open an entry to explore
    the details needed to understand and reproduce the workflow.
  find_title: How to Find an Experiment
  find_intro: Start with a category or go straight to a method you know.
  find_steps:
  - title: Browse by Category
    text: Select a category to explore experiments from a specific area of our work.
      Molecular Biology and Cell Biology contain general techniques; the other categories
      follow distinct ORCA experimental lines.
  - title: Search by Name or ID
    text: Use the search bar to find an experiment by its title, identifier, or related
      keywords. Search and category filters work together.
  - title: Open and Navigate
    text: Select an experiment card to read the full procedure. Use the section navigation
      to jump directly to materials, steps, notes, or other available sections.
  - title: Follow the Connections
    text: Related links connect an experiment to the corresponding work elsewhere
      in the Wiki. When available, source files and references provide additional
      detail.
  read_title: How to Read an Experiment
  read_intro: Each entry is organized into practical sections so you can quickly locate
    the information you need. The sections below explain what each part contributes.
  read_modules:
  - title: Experiment Information
    lead: Identify the experiment at a glance.
    text: Find its ID, category, and any documented details about the experimental
      system, relevant materials, or instruments.
  - title: Purpose
    lead: Understand what the method is for.
    text: This section briefly explains the task the experiment is intended to accomplish
      and where the procedure fits into our work.
  - title: Materials & Equipment
    lead: Check what you need before starting.
    text: Find the required reagents, consumables, equipment, and — when documented
      — suppliers, catalog numbers, and specifications.
  - title: Before You Start
    lead: Prepare the experiment correctly.
    text: Review prerequisites, sample preparation, setup requirements, and other
      essential considerations before beginning the procedure.
  - title: Workflow
    lead: See the overall sequence first.
    text: For longer procedures, a concise workflow overview maps the major stages
      before you read each step in detail.
  - title: Procedure
    lead: Follow the method step by step.
    text: Numbered instructions provide the experimental sequence and the documented
      conditions, quantities, timings, and settings needed to carry it out.
  - title: Expected Outcome
    lead: Know what to look for.
    text: Where the source material supports it, this section describes the expected
      product, observable outcome, or practical checkpoint of the procedure.
  - title: Notes & Troubleshooting
    lead: Learn from practical details.
    text: Find source-supported precautions, useful handling notes, and troubleshooting
      guidance that may help avoid common problems.
  - title: Related Work & References
    lead: Trace the experiment in context.
    text: Follow links to relevant ORCA Wiki sections and, when available, consult
      references or the original procedure files.
  clarification_lead: Not every experiment needs every section.
  clarification: Short, routine procedures stay concise, while more involved workflows
    include additional preparation, overview, or troubleshooting information where
    available.
  closing: Ready to explore? Choose a category or search for an experiment below.
