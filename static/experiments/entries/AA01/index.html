Co-transfection for AAV Packaging AA01 | ver 1.2 | Revised 2026-10-03 SUSTech-BIO 2026 iGEM Team Purpose Prepare AAV9 packaging cultures by co-transfecting adherent HEK293T cells with pAAV2/9n, pAdDeltaF6 and one transfer plasmid: MCS-ORCA or MCS-mCherry. Use two 15 cm dishes per AAV preparation. This protocol ends at the harvest handoff to AA02. Materials & Equipment Full name / construct Supplier / catalog no. Label each preparation and its two dishes separately. ORCA and mCherry require separate DNA mixtures; packaging both requires four dishes. Two dishes pooled into one preparation constitute one production batch, not two independent biological replicates. The dose below is an adapted starting condition, not a validated optimum for these AAV constructs. It uses the lower Lipofectamine 3000 dose from the manufacturer scaling approach. Assess cell health and delivery before treating it as an established production recipe. [1, 2] Procedure A. Prepare the two packaging dishes 1. Seed two 15 cm dishes of healthy 293T cells per AAV preparation, aiming for 70-90% confluence at transfection. Use approximately 30 mL complete medium per dish before transfection and incubate at 37°C with 5% CO2. Adjust the seeding number from observed growth in this format. [1, 6] 2. Check even attachment and morphology on the transfection day. Proceed when both dishes are healthy and within the intended confluence range. Keep approximately 30 mL growth medium in each receiving dish before adding complexes. B. Calculate the three-plasmid mixture 3. Allocate 40 µg total DNA per dish using a starting molar ratio of pAAV2/9n : pAdDeltaF6 : transfer plasmid = 1:1:1. The equal-molar approach is adapted from Addgene AAV production; it is not a supplier-validated optimum for this Lipofectamine workflow. [5] Let L_R, L_H and L_T be the complete lengths (bp) of the packaging, helper and transfer plasmids. Set S = L_R + L_H + L_T. Calculate masses per dish as shown below; recalculate when switching transfer constructs. Plasmid DNA per dish (µg) DNA for two dishes (µg) pAAV2/9n 40 × L_R / S 80 × L_R / S pAdDeltaF6 40 × L_H / S 80 × L_H / S MCS-ORCA OR MCS-mCherry 40 × L_T / S 80 × L_T / S Total DNA 40 80 4. Convert each DNA mass to a stock volume: volume (µL) = mass (µg) / concentration (µg/µL). Divide ng/µL by 1,000 before using this equation. Use full plasmid lengths, including backbone, rather than insert or packaged-genome lengths. AA01 | v1.2 2

The manufacturer lower DNA and lipid inputs scale to approximately 37.7 µg and 58.4 µL, rounded here to 40 µg and 60 µL; each 0.5 mL Opti-MEM portion scales to 1.35 mL. P3000 is 2 µL per µg DNA. Check the actual dish growth area. [1, 2, 6, 7] 5. Prepare tube A with the stated Opti-MEM, all three calculated DNA volumes and P3000. Mix gently. Prepare tube B separately with Opti-MEM and Lipofectamine 3000; mix the diluted lipid with a brief 2-3 s vortex. [1] 6. Add A to B, mix gently by pipetting and incubate for 10-15 min at room temperature. Keep preparation and addition times consistent. [1] The table specifies Opti-MEM added, not final tube volume. DNA, P3000 and lipid add further volume. For a two-dish mixture, divide the entire combined mixture equally between the two dishes; each half contains one full dish dose. No extra pipetting allowance is included. D. Add complexes and replace medium 7. Add one dish-equivalent of complexes slowly along the inner wall, distributing additions around the dish without directing a jet onto the monolayer. Immediately mix by gentle forward-backward and left-right movement. Return both dishes to the incubator and record addition as t = 0. 8. At 18 h after transfection, gently remove the spent medium and replace it with 20 mL prewarmed fresh complete medium per dish. Add against the wall and retain the attached monolayer. Two dishes require 40 mL fresh medium in total. The 18 h exchange and 20 mL replacement volume are the team-specified workflow. They are not requirements of the Lipofectamine instructions. At this reduced culture volume, maintain even coverage and inspect pH, evaporation and cell condition. E. Continue culture to the harvest handoff 9. Continue incubation at 37°C with 5% CO2 and inspect morphology and medium appearance daily. For MCS-mCherry, document fluorescence if the construct expresses mCherry in 293T cells; fluorescence alone does not establish packaged-vector yield. AA01 | v1.2 3

10. Proceed to AA02 at 72 h after transfection, equivalent to 54 h after the medium exchange. Keep the two dishes of each preparation identified together and separate from other constructs. Retain culture material until the cell/supernatant collection route in AA02 is defined. Timing: count all intervals from complex addition (t = 0). Replace medium at t = 18 h and harvest at t = 72 h, leaving 54 h of culture after the exchange. This is the team-specified schedule. p.s. Equal molar input does not mean equal DNA mass. A larger helper plasmid requires more mass than a shorter transfer plasmid to supply the same number of molecules. QP1174 is the catalog number of the pAAV-MCS source backbone, not of the completed ORCA construct. For QP3064, retain the full name pAAV-MCS-mCherry-T2A-Luc, including T2A-Luc, in material and sample records. Keep both dishes of one preparation on the same recipe. If comparing reagent doses, identify the conditions separately rather than pooling them as one uniform preparation. Use slow wall-directed liquid addition and thorough gentle cross mixing during transfection and medium exchange; 293T monolayers detach easily. Expected Outcome Obtain two identifiable transfected cultures per AAV preparation, with documented cell condition, DNA inputs, reagent doses and timing. mCherry fluorescence can support expression in producer cells. Packaging success, vector-genome titer and functional transduction require the downstream AA02-AA04 workflow; no yield or efficiency is assumed. Troubleshooting Observed problem Check first Action Cells detach during Initial attachment, pipette direction Add against the wall more slowly; avoid addition or exchange and liquid flow direct jets and vigorous dish movement. Marked rounding or Untreated culture, DNA purity and Check for culture problems first. If toxicity is death after transfection dose calculations treatment-specific, assess a lower lipid dose at fixed DNA in a separate pilot. Weak mCherry signal Transfer identity, promoter, Verify the expression readout and plasmid with healthy cells concentration and complex first; assess delivery in a separate matched preparation pilot. Do not infer AAV titer from fluorescence. Different appearance Confluence, equal mixture division Standardize seeding, mixing and dosing; between the two dishes and addition timing record discordance before pooling. Rapid medium yellowing Culture density, contamination, Check the cause and cell condition promptly. or loss of coverage evaporation and dish level Record any necessary departure from the 20 mL schedule.
