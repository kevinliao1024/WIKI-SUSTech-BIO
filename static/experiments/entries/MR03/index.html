The reference setup uses the existing HEK293T / miR-124-3p mimic workflow: 30 nM mimic in 1 mL final volume per 12-well plate well. Check the oligonucleotide identity, amount, diluent and labeled stock concentration. Use the delivered nmol amount or the supplier’s conversion for that exact duplex. Do not treat OD as a universal molar unit. Prepare serum-free Opti-MEM for complex formation. Receiving wells may contain serum. Antibiotic-free growth medium follows the earlier project workflow; RNAiMAX itself does not universally require antibiotics to be excluded. [1, 2] Procedure A. Prepare the oligonucleotide stocks 1. Centrifuge the unopened lyophilized mimic tube below 4,000 × g for 4–5 min to collect the powder. Reconstitute target mimic and NC separately with DEPC-treated water. Pipette gently to dissolve, then briefly spin down. [5] For GenePharma double-stranded mimics, the manual specifies 125 µL DEPC-treated water per OD to make a nominal 20 µM stock: a 2 OD tube therefore receives 250 µL. If the batch certificate specifies the amount in nmol, calculate volume (µL) = nmol × 1000 / 20 and follow the batch-specific value. This OD conversion does not apply to single-stranded products. [3, 5] 2. Label stocks with identity, 20 µM concentration and preparation date. Divide into approximately 10–15 µL working aliquots, seal securely and store at -80°C. Minimize repeated freeze-thaw cycles; the supplied manual recommends using dissolved aliquots within 3 months. [1, 5] B. Calculate each formulation 3. For each target or control formulation, calculate all ingredients from the same number of well-equivalents. Use 20 µM = 20 pmol/µL; 30 pmol divided by 1 mL final culture volume gives 30 nM. C. Form and add the complexes 4. Inspect attachment, confluence and morphology. Adjust each receiving well to 900 µL of fresh growth medium, keeping the composition identical across groups. [1] 5. Prepare Tube A by diluting the mimic or NC in Opti-MEM to the calculated total volume. Prepare Tube B separately by diluting RNAiMAX in Opti-MEM. Mix gently. [1] 6. Add Tube A to its corresponding Tube B, mix gently and incubate for 5 min at room temperature. Start timing after the solutions have been combined. [1] 7. Add 100 µL of the appropriate complex mixture to each well. Distribute it across the medium, then gently rock the plate forward-backward and left-right to mix. [1] For a reagent-only mock, replace the 1.5 µL oligonucleotide stock with the same diluent while keeping 3 µL RNAiMAX and 100 µL total complex-preparation volume. Untreated wells receive 1 mL growth medium without lipid or mimic. These controls answer different questions. D. Incubate and assess 8. Return HEK293T cultures to 37°C and 5% CO2. Record cell morphology around 24 h and collect the planned functional readout within the selected 24–72 h window. Compare all groups at the same elapsed time. [1] RNAiMAX does not require immediate complex removal. For assays continuing beyond 24 h, GenePharma suggests replacing the medium at about 24 h to support viability. Apply the same schedule to all groups; a second plasmid transfection follows C04 and the experiment-specific design. [2, 4] 9. Assess cell health and a target-relevant functional readout against the matched NC. For mature-miRNA measurement, use small-RNA-preserving extraction such as MR01 and an appropriate miRNA RT assay; interpret abundance together with function. p.s. Count the 100 µL complex mixture within the 1 mL final volume. Adding it to an existing 1 mL of medium produces 1.1 mL and approximately 27.3 nM, not 30 nM. Opti-MEM in the recipe is added to a defined total: 48.5 µL plus 1.5 µL RNA, and 47 µL plus 3 µL lipid. Adding 50 µL Opti-MEM to each tube would change the dispensing volumes. Use the matched mimic NC. The delivery also lists a separate 0.50 OD “Negative control”; its function is not established by that label, so it is not substituted for the 2 OD mimic NC. [5] Use functional evidence to judge miRNA activity. A stronger miRNA qPCR signal or uptake of a fluorescent control alone does not establish the intended target effect. For experiments involving a second transfection, monitor the combined stress and include controls that receive the same treatment schedule. A broad decrease in cell health or signal cannot be assigned specifically to an MRE. MR03 | v1.1 3

Expected Outcome Cells should remain suitable for the planned assay, with morphology and viability documented alongside target and control identities, RNA dose, lipid dose and timing. A mimic effect is assessed relative to its matched NC and an appropriate functional control; no fixed suppression percentage is assumed. [4] For an MRE reporter experiment, compare the mimic effect on constructs with and without the relevant MRE. This comparison helps distinguish sequence-dependent regulation from general treatment effects.
