[1] Materials & Equipment Item Supplier / identifier Use PrimeScript RT reagent Kit with gDNA Eraser Takara / RR047A DNA removal and cDNA (Perfect Real Time) synthesis Purified total RNA Sample-specific Measured concentration; assess integrity RNase-free tubes and filter tips Axygen tips; laboratory Reaction setup supplies Pipettes, ice rack and microcentrifuge Eppendorf centrifuge; Dispensing and collection laboratory pipettes and ice rack Thermal cycler Same laboratory thermal 42°C, 37°C and 85°C stages cycler as M01 Before You Start Use 850 ng total RNA per reaction. This team-selected input is within the manufacturer’s 1 µg limit for the TB Green recipe. [1] Calculate RNA volume V (µL) = 850 / RNA concentration (ng/µL). V must be ≤7 µL, requiring RNA at ≥121.43 ng/µL. If RNA is more dilute, concentrate or re-extract it and remeasure before setup; do not exceed the 10 µL DNA-removal volume. Keep kit stocks at -20°C. Vortex both buffers and briefly spin; collect viscous enzymes by a short spin and pipette slowly. Assemble reactions on ice. [1] Include a matched no-RT control where DNA could amplify: process the same RNA through DNA removal, then replace the 1 µL RT enzyme with water. Include a no-template control in the downstream PCR. Procedure A. Remove genomic DNA 1. Combine the following for each sample. Use each sample’s measured RNA concentration to calculate its RNA and water volumes. [1] Component Volume per reaction 5× gDNA Eraser Buffer 2 µL gDNA Eraser 1 µL Total RNA (850 ng) V = 850 / concentration (ng/µL) RNase-free water (7 - V) µL Total 10 µL 2. Incubate at 42°C for 2 min, then place at 4°C while preparing the next stage. [1] B. Synthesize cDNA 3. Prepare 10 µL of RT addition mix per sample, excluding the treated RNA. Add it to the entire 10 µL DNA-removal reaction, mix gently and start the program promptly. [1] Component Volume DNA-removal reaction 10 µL 5× PrimeScript Buffer 2 (for Real Time) 4 µL PrimeScript RT Enzyme Mix I 1 µL RT Primer Mix 1 µL RNase-free water 4 µL Final volume 20 µL 4. Run 37°C for 15 min, followed by 85°C for 5 sec and a 4°C hold. [1] C. Store and use cDNA 5. Store products at or below -20°C for longer-term use. For qPCR, keep carried-over RT reaction at ≤10% of the PCR volume; this corresponds to ≤2 µL undiluted product in 20 µL PCR. Record any dilution. [1] M10 | v1.1 2
