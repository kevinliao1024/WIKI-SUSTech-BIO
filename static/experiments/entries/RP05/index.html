Fluorescence Measurement Using a Microplate Reader RP05 | ver 1.1 | Revised 2026-10-14 SUSTech-BIO 2026 iGEM Team Purpose Measure reporter fluorescence directly from adherent 293T cells in clear Thermo Scientific Nunc 12-well culture plates using a Varioskan LUX reader and SkanIt Software. Replace culture medium with 1 mL PBS per well, then acquire multipoint fluorescence from below the plate at 37°C without shaking. Single-point readings are generally unsuitable as a representative whole-well measurement for this adherent-cell workflow: uneven cell coverage or reporter distribution can make one location misleading. Use multipoint bottom reading with a consistent sampling pattern. Complete the reader setup before exchanging medium for PBS. Keep the time from PBS addition to measurement short and comparable across samples; PBS is the reading liquid in this workflow, not a replacement for prolonged cell culture. Inspect the adherent layer before washing. 293T cells can lift off during liquid exchange: use slow aspiration and wall-directed addition throughout. Avoid touching the cell layer, directing a liquid jet onto it or shaking the plate. Procedure A. Set up the reader and measurement session 1. Switch on the reader and open SkanIt. Select the connected Varioskan LUX. Enable the instrument incubator, set 37°C and wait for the displayed current temperature to reach the target before measuring. [1] 2. Create a measurement session and open Plate Layout. Select the matching 12-well plate template. The archived session uses 'Thermo NUNC, Multidish, 12-well'; verify that it matches the actual plate. Check the installed 6-48-well adapter for a plate without a lid and retain the software's adapter compatibility check. [1,3] 3. Select the sample and blank wells to be measured and record their identities in the plate map. Use the same acquisition settings for samples and both blank types. 4. Add a Fluorescence measurement step for each required reporter channel and enter the excitation and emission wavelengths below. These values are the local acquisition settings shown in the supplied screenshots, not universal spectral maxima for all fluorescent-protein variants. [3] Channel Excitation (nm) Emission (nm) BFP readout, when present 405 460 EGFP 488 510 mCherry 587 611 5. For every fluorescence step, select Bottom reading, set measurement time to 100 ms, excitation bandwidth to 5 nm and Dynamic range to Auto. Enable Multipoint measurement and choose All to sample the well across the available point pattern. Apply the same pattern to every measured well and channel. Do not add a shaking step. Figure 1. Multipoint settings in the laboratory SkanIt session. Enable Multipoint measurement and select All; the orange points show the selected sampling pattern. The EGFP step is illustrated. Repeat this setting for every required fluorescence channel. [3] B. Exchange medium for PBS without disturbing cells 6. Aspirate the culture medium slowly from the side of each well. Keep the tip away from the adherent layer and avoid scraping the bottom. Do not leave the cells uncovered while preparing the next action. 7. Gently rinse the adherent layer with PBS, using enough liquid to cover the well bottom. Add PBS slowly down the well wall and let it spread without forceful mixing. Gently aspirate the rinse from the side. Avoid repeated washing that strips loosely attached 293T cells. 8. Immediately add 1 mL fresh PBS to each sample well, again slowly along the wall. Keep the cell layer covered and the reading volume the same across samples. 9. Prepare the two blank types on the same plate as defined below. Keep their well identities distinct in the exported data. Well type Contents during reading Role Sample Adherent 293T cells + 1 mL PBS Cell-associated reporter fluorescence plus background. PBS-only blank 1 mL of the same PBS; no cells Matched liquid/plate background for later processing. Empty-well blank No cells and no added liquid Diagnostic baseline for the empty plate under the reading setup. 10. Inspect the cell layer after exchange. Record visible lifting, floating sheets or loss of cells; flag affected wells because their fluorescence no longer represents the intended adherent sample. Check for bubbles without shaking or pipetting across the monolayer. RP05 | v1.1 3

C. Read the original culture plate 11. Remove the plate lid and check that the outside of the clear bottom is clean and dry. Seat the original culture plate correctly in the tray, with its orientation matching the plate map. Do not transfer the cells to another plate. 12. Confirm incubator On, 37°C, bottom reading and multipoint acquisition in every required channel, with no shaking step. Start the measurement and retain the complete acquisition session. 13. Review the well and point results for missing measurements, over-range warnings and strongly uneven spatial signals. Check these against the plate map and the post-wash cell appearance before interpreting a low or inconsistent readout. D. Save and export before processing 14. Save the native .skax session. In Report, select the raw results for all measured channels together with plate layout, protocol settings, instrument information and the run log, then export to Excel. Retain the per-point results as well as any displayed well summary; check that the exported file actually contains the intended point data. [3] Figure 2. Report export controls in SkanIt 7.0.2 RE. Select the required report contents, then use the Excel export control. Save the native session separately so that the acquisition settings and point results remain recoverable. [3] 15. Keep the exported raw data unchanged and perform background correction and reporter analysis afterward. For sample wells containing PBS, use the PBS-only blank as the matched acellular background; retain empty wells for diagnostic comparison rather than subtracting both blank types in succession. Record the point-to-well summary rule used in later processing. 16. Eject and cover the plate after reading, then handle the samples according to the experiment's endpoint plan and follow the local reader shutdown procedure. p.s. Handle 293T cells gently at every liquid-exchange step. Aspirate from the side and add PBS down the wall; forceful addition, scraping or plate shaking can detach cells and create an apparent loss of fluorescence. Use multipoint acquisition to represent the adherent well. A central single-point reading can miss patches of cells or overrepresent a bright patch. Keep the sampling pattern fixed across conditions, rather than selecting only the brightest positions. [2] RP05 | v1.1 4

Do not double-subtract PBS-only and empty-well signals. Neither blank removes cellular autofluorescence; retain any experiment-specific cell controls in later analysis. Measurement points within one well are spatial readings, not independent culture replicates. Preserve point data and independent well identities. Expected Outcome Obtain multipoint fluorescence data for the required channels, an identifiable plate map, both blank types and a saved acquisition session. The 293T layer should remain attached. The exported data support later background correction and well-level reporter comparisons. Signal detectability depends on the sample and controls; fluorescence alone does not establish RNA-editing efficiency.
