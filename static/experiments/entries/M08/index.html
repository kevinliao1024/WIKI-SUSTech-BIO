Highly fibrous or RNase-rich tissues require the manufacturer's sample-specific modifications; consult the linked manual before using those samples. [1, 2] Procedure Sample preparation 1. Remove culture medium. Lyse adherent cells directly, or pellet harvested/suspension cells and remove supernatant. Add RL according to the table and vortex until clumps disappear. For tissue, homogenize in RL on ice, or grind under liquid nitrogen before adding RL. [1] Column purification Follow the sequence below at 15-25°C. Every column spin uses 13,400 × g. [1] 2. Pass lysate through the gDNA-filter column for 30 s; retain filtrate and discard this column. Mix filtrate with the specified ethanol. Transfer the entire mixture, including any flocculent material, onto the RNA column. [1] 3. Complete RNA binding and washes in this order. Discard flow-through after each spin; retain the RNA column. [1] Stage Addition to RNA column Spin at 13,400 × g Binding Entire ethanol/filtrate mixture 30 s Wash 1 700 µL RW1 30 s Wash 2 700 µL ethanol-prepared RW2 30 s Wash 3 500 µL ethanol-prepared RW2 2 min Conditional dry spin None; empty collection tube first if residual liquid 1 min remains or the column touches flow-through Elution 4. Move the RNA column into a clean RNase-free 1.5 mL tube. Apply 50-200 µL RNase-free water centrally without touching the membrane. Wait 1 min, then spin for 1 min. Retain eluate for downstream use or storage at -85 to -65°C. [1] M08 | v1.1 2

p.s. Keep filtrate at the gDNA-removal stage, but keep the column during RNA binding and washing. Choose centrifuge settings by relative centrifugal force. The manual's paired 12,000 rpm value is rotor-dependent. For an optional yield improvement, prewarm elution water to 65°C and extend membrane incubation to 2-5 min before spinning. [1] Expected Outcome The final collection tube contains purified total RNA. Check concentration, purity and, when required, integrity before M10 reverse transcription. Record the input and elution volume alongside measurements; recovery varies with sample type and condition. [2] RNA recovery alone does not establish absence of DNA. For expression assays, assess residual DNA with an appropriate no-reverse-transcriptase control.
