Cell Seeding & Transfection C04 | ver 1.1 | Revised 2026-10-03 SUSTech-BIO 2026 iGEM Team Purpose Seed adherent cells at a defined density and deliver plasmid DNA using Lipofectamine 3000 and P3000. Our 293T reference is 300,000 cells per 12-well plate well, usually reaching approximately 70% confluence after 18 h. The plate conversions and reagent recipes below provide starting conditions for systematic setup. Prewarm the culture medium. Opti-MEM is used to prepare complexes, not to replace all of the growth medium. Keep medium composition consistent across groups. The product permits serum and antibiotics in the receiving culture medium; it does not require an automatic medium change after addition. [1] Procedure A. Calculate the seeding number 1. Use 300,000 viable 293T cells per well of a 12-well plate as the reference. For another format, calculate cells per well = 300,000 × (target well growth area / reference 12-well growth area). The example below uses Corning reference areas: 3.8 cm² for a 12-well well. Replace both areas with the specifications of the plates actually used. These are area-scaled starting cell numbers, not measured outcomes for every format. [2] Equal cell numbers per cm² do not guarantee equal confluence after 18 h; inspect each format before transfection. 2. Prepare a well-dispersed cell suspension using C01 and determine its viable-cell concentration C (cells/mL). Calculate suspension volume per well = required cells / C. Add medium to reach the selected plating volume; the cell suspension is included in this volume. Example: at C = 1,000,000 viable cells/mL, a 12-well well needs 0.30 mL suspension plus 0.70 mL medium for 1.0 mL total. For multiple wells, prepare the required total plus a modest, explicitly calculated pipetting allowance. B. Seed and check the culture 3. Remix the cell suspension gently before dispensing, as cells settle during plating. Seed the calculated number into each well, then distribute cells by gentle forward-backward and left-right movement. Avoid circular swirling that concentrates cells unevenly. 4. Incubate 293T cells at 37°C with 5% CO2 for approximately 18 h. Inspect several fields per well: cells should be attached, evenly distributed and approximately 70% confluent in our routine setup. Use actual cell condition to decide readiness; do not transfect solely because 18 h has elapsed. C04 | v1.1 2

For other cell lines, establish a small seeding-density pilot at the intended transfection time and select a density from observed attachment and confluence. The general manufacturer target is 70-90% at transfection. [1] Do not assume the 293T cell number or timing transfers directly. C. Calculate DNA, Opti-MEM, P3000 and lipid 5. Set the total plasmid DNA mass D per well. For co-transfection, D is the sum of all plasmids. Calculate each plasmid volume as mass (µg) / stock concentration (µg/µL); if concentration is reported in ng/µL, divide it by 1,000 first. Keep total DNA comparable between experimental groups. D. Prepare A and B separately, then combine 7. Prepare solution A by adding the required plasmid stock(s) to the stated Opti-MEM volume, then adding P3000. Mix gently by pipetting. Prepare one DNA composition per tube or master mix. [1] 8. Prepare solution B in a separate tube: add the selected Lipofectamine 3000 dose to its stated Opti-MEM volume and mix. A brief 2-3 s vortex is specified for diluted lipid. [1] Do not add concentrated lipid directly to concentrated DNA. 9. Add solution A to solution B and gently pipette to mix without frothing. Combine the complete per-well quantities; incubate for 10-15 min at room temperature, then add to cells. [1] Keep this interval consistent across conditions. Volume convention: the table lists Opti-MEM to ADD to each tube. DNA, P3000 and lipid contribute additional volume. Equal Opti-MEM portions therefore do not produce exactly equal final A/B volumes. Transfer the whole prepared per-well mixture, rather than removing a nominal 100 µL and leaving part of a 12-well dose behind. E. Add along the wall and mix the plate 10. Slowly add the prepared complexes dropwise along the inner wall of each well, allowing the liquid to enter the culture medium gently. Distribute additions along the accessible wall rather than delivering one forceful bolus. Keep the tip away from the cell layer; do not jet liquid onto the monolayer. 11. Immediately after addition, thoroughly but gently mix the plate in a cross pattern: move it forward and backward, then left and right, repeating until the liquid is evenly distributed. Keep the plate level enough to prevent spills. Do not substitute a single circular swirl, vigorous shaking or pipetting over the attached cells. 12. Return the plate to the incubator. Record addition time, actual confluence, DNA mass, stock volumes, reagent doses and any detachment. Inspect morphology the next day and use the time point defined by the downstream assay for the readout. Assess transfection together with cell health. p.s. The 300,000-cell reference means cells seeded, not an inferred cell count at 70% confluence. Record the observed confluence after 18 h; do not calculate a doubling time from this single reference. Label tubes by contents: A = DNA + P3000; B = Lipofectamine 3000. The letters alone are not sufficient because other protocols may use the opposite convention. Do not double the DNA dose when reading the manufacturer's two-condition optimization scheme. Each test well receives the same DNA mass and one selected lipid dose. Keep the interval between mixing A/B and adding to cells consistent. Prepare manageable batches so later wells do not wait much longer than earlier wells. Slow wall-directed addition protects the monolayer, but still requires immediate, thorough cross mixing to distribute the complexes throughout the medium. Expected Outcome In the routine 12-well 293T setup, expect approximately 70% confluence after 18 h, with attached cells distributed across the well. Area-scaled formats require visual confirmation. Successful delivery should produce the intended assay signal relative to controls while retaining acceptable cell morphology and survival; no fixed transfection-efficiency threshold is assumed.
