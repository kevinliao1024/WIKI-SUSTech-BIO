ASO Treatment AS01 | ver 1.1 | Revised 2026-10-03 SUSTech-BIO 2026 iGEM Team Purpose Deliver antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs) into adherent cells by reverse transfection with Lipofectamine RNAiMAX. Place ASO-lipid complexes in empty culture wells before adding a freshly prepared cell suspension. The reference formulation uses 30 nM ASO in HEK293T cells. It is an adapted starting condition, not an optimized dose: 30 nM is the upper end of IDT’s general 1-30 nM starting range for lipid-mediated ASO delivery. RNAiMAX handling is adapted from its small-RNA protocol. Prepare stocks and calculate the formulation 1. Briefly centrifuge lyophilized ASO tubes before opening. Dissolve target ASO and NC separately in the supplier-recommended diluent, using the batch-specific molar amount. Prepare labeled 20 µM working stocks for the table below; aliquot and store according to the supplied product instructions. For a 20 µM stock, diluent volume (µL) = amount (nmol) × 1000 / 20. For an existing stock, use C1V1 = C2V2. Do not apply a generic OD-to-nmol conversion to a modified ASO. 2. Calculate each target or NC formulation from the same number of well-equivalents. The table is a reference recipe at 30 nM; all Opti-MEM entries are actual added volumes, and each A or B mixture has the indicated total. B. Prepare cells and form complexes 3. Detach cells using C01, neutralize the dissociation reagent and prepare a uniform suspension in growth medium without added antibiotics. Count viable cells and adjust the concentration so that 450 µL or 900 µL contains the selected cell input. Keep the suspension ready for prompt dispensing. 4. Prepare mixture A by diluting ASO or NC in serum-free Opti-MEM. Gently mix RNAiMAX before preparing mixture B in a separate tube. [1] 5. Combine equal volumes of A and B, mix gently, and dispense 50 µL per 24-well well or 100 µL per 12-well well into the empty plate. Allow 5 min at room temperature from the time A and B are combined. This timing follows MAN0007825; use small batches to keep timing comparable. [1] For the mock, replace the ASO stock volume with its diluent while retaining the lipid dose and total complex volume. Prepare untreated wells at the same final volume and viable-cell input, without ASO or lipid. C. Add cells and assess the response 6. Gently resuspend settled cells, then add 450 µL cell suspension to each 24-well complex or 900 µL to each 12-well complex. Rock the plate forward-backward and left-right to distribute cells evenly. The cells are added after complex formation. [3] 7. Incubate HEK293T cells at 37°C and 5% CO2. Inspect attachment, morphology and growth at approximately 24 h. No routine immediate medium change is required; keep any later medium replacement consistent across groups. [1] 8. Collect the planned readout at a matched time within an initial 24-72 h assessment window. [3] Measure target RNA and, where relevant, protein or reporter output. Record viability alongside these measurements; retain raw images or data and the treatment-to-readout interval. p.s. Count complexes within the final culture volume. The 450 µL or 900 µL addition is the cell suspension, not extra medium to add after plating cells. Pool well-equivalents when 0.75 µL ASO cannot be dispensed reliably. Prepare only the required working mixture and distribute equal complex volumes; do not add a second dose of neat ASO or RNAiMAX. Keep ASO dose, lipid dose and cell input independently adjustable. When comparing ASO doses, hold lipid amount constant initially rather than increasing both together. [4] For tag-dependent regulation, compare constructs with and without the ASO recognition tag under the same ASO/NC schedule. Expected Outcome Cells should attach and remain suitable for the selected assay. An active ASO may reduce target RNA or its downstream output relative to NC, with cell health assessed in parallel. No fixed knockdown percentage is assumed. Preserve ASO/NC identities, stock concentrations, cell inputs, lipid amounts, treatment times and raw readouts.
