Before You Start Check the medium and culture Use the Servicebio G1711 medium as supplied; gently mix before use and do not add serum, DMSO or basal medium. Decide whether to use a freezing container Situation Choice and trade-off Servicebio G1711: routine A controlled-rate freezing container is not required by the Servicebio instructions. stock Place aliquoted vials directly at −80°C. Our stocks remain in the −80°C freezer. [6] Considering a container with Adding a container changes the cooling profile. The instructions do not establish a Servicebio recovery advantage; use it only as a deliberately evaluated alternative, with density and fill held constant. Conventional slow-cooling Use an appropriate container or controlled-rate freezer, typically around formulation −1°C/min. This comparison is not an extra stage in the Servicebio procedure. [2, 5] New, sensitive or valuable Keep a recoverable backup while assessing a representative vial. A different stock cell-specific formulation or cooling protocol may be needed if direct freezing gives poor recovery. For the optional slow-cooling comparison, a freezing container is a passive device, not an electronically programmed freezer. An ordinary cryobox does not provide a controlled cooling rate. Mr. Frosty specifically starts at room temperature, uses 100% isopropanol and requires alcohol replacement after five uses; alcohol-free devices have their own instructions. These preparations are unnecessary for the main direct-freezing route. [5] Procedure A. Collect and count 1. Harvest adherent cells using C01: wash, detach gently, neutralize the dissociation reagent and collect in complete medium. For a suspension line, collect the suspension directly using its established harvesting procedure. 2. Mix gently, take a representative sample and measure viable cell concentration and total suspension volume. Record cell identity, passage and pre-freeze viability. Calculate the total viable cell number before choosing the number of vials. 3. Pellet cells gently and remove the supernatant without disturbing the pellet. The Servicebio instructions state 800–1200 rpm for 3–5 min; rpm depends on rotor radius and is not interchangeable with × g. The C01 condition of 200 × g for 5 min may be retained as a laboratory adaptation where effective for the cell line, rather than presented as a conversion of the supplier's setting. [2, 6] B. Resuspend and aliquot 4. Calculate the final freezing-suspension volume: V (mL) = total viable cells / target viable cells per mL. For example, 1.2 × 10^7 viable cells at 3 × 10^6/mL require 4 mL final suspension, giving four 1 mL aliquots. This is arithmetic, not a confirmed team density. 5. Gently mix the ready-to-use G1711 medium, then resuspend the pellet in the calculated volume. Proceed promptly; no additional warming or equilibration hold is specified. [6] C02 | v1.3 3

6. Dispense 1 mL per compatible cryovial, as recommended by Servicebio. Mix gently between aliquots to limit settling; record cells/mL and cells/vial. Close vials securely and proceed promptly to freezing. [6] C. Freeze and store at −80°C 7. Place the cryovials directly in a stable −80°C freezer using a suitable vial holder. Do not use a controlled-rate freezing container for this standard route, and do not add 4°C or −20°C holding stages. Keep the freezer temperature stable and minimize door opening. [6] 8. Keep the frozen vials in the −80°C freezer. Record the freezing date and box/vial position, and record storage duration when a vial is retrieved for recovery. Minimize unnecessary handling and temperature fluctuations. 9. Recover a representative vial using C03 for compatible routine adherent lines. Dilute the thawed suspension in complete medium before centrifugation: the Servicebio medium is viscous and should not be centrifuged undiluted. Assess viable recovery, attachment and subsequent growth/morphology. Keep the original culture or backup stock until the new batch has passed recovery assessment. [6] p.s. Prepare labels, the vial holder and freezer space before resuspending cells in freezing medium. Avoid leaving the final tubes waiting while setting up the next step. Use G1711 as supplied. It is DMSO-free; do not add DMSO, serum or basal medium to this ready-to-use reagent. Gently mix the reagent before resuspending the cells. [6] Higher density is not automatically better: clumps impair counting and even aliquoting. A sparse vial may also yield too few recoverable cells. Compare recovery under the same conditions before narrowing the range. For bank qualification, record the G1711 lot, viable cells per vial, fill volume, cooling route and thawing observations. Avoid changing density and cooling route simultaneously when diagnosing a poor batch. Expected Outcome A qualified stock should recover viable cells that resume the expected morphology and growth after thawing. Report viable recovery as recovered viable cells divided by the viable cells originally frozen, using a defined counting time; assess later attachment and expansion separately. No fixed recovery percentage or Servicebio-specific optimum is claimed. C02 | v1.3 4
