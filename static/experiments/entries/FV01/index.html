Before You Start Prepare healthy CCF-STTG1 cultures using C01. Prewarm antibiotic-free recovery medium and prepare one labeled 12-well well containing 1 mL per electroporation. This is the manufacturer-based reference format for 200,000 adherent cells, not a claim about a previously performed run. [1] Program the Neon device to 1350 V, 20 ms, 2 pulses. Use Buffer R for this workflow. The pulse settings were supplied by the team; they are not presented as a manufacturer-validated CCF-STTG1 optimum. For plasmid delivery, select a dose within the 0.5-3 µg reference range according to the construct and endpoint. Use measured stock concentration to calculate volume; keep the combined volume of all DNA stocks ≤1 µL. For co-transfection, apply the selected construct ratio within the total dose. [1] Include untreated cells, a pulse-only control and a suitable delivery-positive control when establishing the method. Add an empty-vector or non-targeting control appropriate to the functional comparison. Select the readout time for the expressed product and downstream assay. Procedure A. Define the 10 µL reaction Parameter Per electroporation Loaded cell number 200,000 cells Loaded mixture / tip size 10 µL / 10 µL Neon tip Final cell density 20,000 cells/µL = 2 × 10^7 cells/mL Plasmid DNA 0.5-3 µg reference range; VDNA = dose (µg) / stock concentration (µg/µL); VDNA ≤1 µL [1] Cells in Buffer R + DNA Vcells + VDNA = 10 µL Required cell-stock density 200,000 / Vcells cells/µL Pulse program 1350 V / 20 ms / 2 pulses Recovery reference One 12-well well containing 1 mL antibiotic-free medium [1] 1. Calculate VDNA from the selected dose and measured stock concentration. Prepare the cell suspension in Buffer R at 200,000 / (10 - VDNA) cells/µL. Scale both cells and DNA proportionally for any pipetting excess; each loaded 10 µL must contain 200,000 cells. Calculation example: 1 µg DNA at 1 µg/µL occupies 1 µL. Combine it with 9 µL of cells in Buffer R at approximately 22,222 cells/µL. This gives 200,000 cells in 10 µL. The example is not a measured optimum. FV01 | v1.1 2

B. Prepare cells and the station 2. Detach CCF-STTG1 cells using C01 and count them. Pellet at 100-400 × g for 5 min at room temperature; wash once with Ca2+/Mg2+-free PBS using the same centrifugation conditions. [1] 3. Remove PBS and gently resuspend the pellet in Buffer R at the calculated cell-stock concentration. Keep the suspension uniform for dispensing. [2] 4. Add 3 mL Buffer E to a Neon tube and seat it in the station. Verify 1350 V, 20 ms and 2 pulses on the display. [1] C. Load, pulse and recover 5. Mix the calculated DNA and cell-suspension volumes. Attach a 10 µL Neon tip securely and slowly aspirate 10 µL without bubbles. [1] 6. Seat the pipette vertically in the station and run the program. Once complete, immediately transfer the cells into the prepared well containing 1 mL prewarmed antibiotic-free medium. [1] 7. Distribute cells gently and incubate at 37°C with 5% CO2. Record construct identity, total DNA dose, actual pulse program, cell input, recovery well and any arcing. 8. At the predefined endpoint, assess delivery and viability separately; compare the experimental and control groups before interpreting downstream functional changes. p.s. Buffer R belongs in the cell mixture; Buffer E belongs in the external Neon tube. Do not replace one with the other. [2] Process small batches; avoid holding cells in resuspension buffer beyond 15-30 min. [1] Discard a bubble-containing load and reload fresh mixture before pulsing. [1] Use a new tip and tube for each different plasmid sample; maximum reuse is two runs per tip and ten per tube. [1] Adding material to an already prepared 10 µL cell aliquot dilutes the cell input in the portion actually loaded. Use the final-mixture calculation above. Expected Outcome Assess plasmid delivery and viable-cell recovery separately, then evaluate the intended biological endpoint at the selected time. Compare with the relevant controls before interpreting functional changes; this protocol does not prescribe a universal efficiency or viability threshold. FV01 | v1.1 3
