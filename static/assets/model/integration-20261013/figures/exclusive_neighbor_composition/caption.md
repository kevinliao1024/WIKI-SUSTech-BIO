Neighbor-base composition in three mutually exclusive selected-positive sets: PUF10-only (553 sites), shared PUF10/PUF12 (2,221 sites), and PUF12-only (10,687 sites). Source allele keys are chromosome, position, reference and alternate base without normalization. Every source 101-nt sequence is used; shared sequences were confirmed identical and counted once. The panels show proportions at −1 and +1 relative to the edited C at index50. Both use a 0–1 color scale; annotations are rounded to three decimals, while CSV retains full precision. G is the most common −1 base in all sets (80.470%, 85.322%, 86.095% in row order); A is the most common +1 base (48.825%, 51.148%, 49.368%). The complete joint 16-triplet table is provided separately: GCA is most common in each set (39.783%, 43.404%, 41.817%). These are compositions conditional on the archived positive-site selection. No additional filter, background enrichment test, pseudocount or p-value is used. Selection in one construct only does not mean zero editing in the other. Local context composition does not identify PUF-binding half sites or establish a repeat-extension recruitment mechanism.
