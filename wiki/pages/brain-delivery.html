State vector

Thirty-four states preserve the handoffs.

The state vector keeps delivery, editor expression, target editing and three off-target mechanisms separate. This prevents a single fitted output from hiding which biological step limits a design.

ODE construction

Fluxes map directly to biological assumptions.

The full right-hand side is the sum of absorption, distribution, brain-barrier transport, intracellular expression and multi-site editing modules.

Binding
Mass-action association, with a separate dissociation flux.
Catalysis
Complex is converted into a codon-specific edited state.
Competition
Codon 112 and 158 pathways share substrate and editor abundance.

The same structure is used for codon 158 and sequential editing. Off-target burden is reported as local bystander + PUF mismatch + deaminase background.

Interpretation boundaries

What must be resolved before stronger claims.

Normalized rates

Several transport and editing values are effective priors, not direct measurements.

Hypothetical editor

The leading ProAPOBEC candidate and its multipliers are placeholders pending sequence selection.

No clinical calibration

The model compares designs; it cannot prescribe a patient dose or efficacy.

Validation required

Linked 112/158 amplicons, expression assays and mechanistic controls are still required.
