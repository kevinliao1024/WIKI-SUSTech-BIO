Priority model

Four evidence layers, one ranked queue.

Available components are bounded, weighted and combined using a geometric mean. Missing components are removed and the remaining weights are renormalized.

50%Sequence

Motif similarity

Mismatch count and optional position-specific weights.

20%Structure

RNA accessibility

Optional RNAplfold unpaired probability around the motif.

20%Expression

Tissue evidence

GTEx-style expression matched by stable gene identifiers.

10%Consequence

Annotation context

Region, splice proximity and potential editable bases.

priority = 100 × ∏ available_componentnormalized_weight

Interpretation ladder

Candidate does not mean confirmed off-target.

LEVEL 1

Sequence candidate

A transcript contains a sufficiently similar motif.

LEVEL 2

Context-supported

Expression, accessibility or annotation raises validation priority.

LEVEL 3

Potential editable base

An editor-compatible base lies in the configured window.

REQUIRED

Experimental evidence

Binding and editing still need to be measured.

The integrated score is a heuristic prioritization score—not a calibrated probability.
