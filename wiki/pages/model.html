Model-guided engineering

Ask, simulate, measure, revise.

  1. 01Expose an uncertain parameter or competing design.
  2. 02Compare predictions under explicit assumptions.
  3. 03Select the measurement with the greatest decision value.
  4. 04Use new evidence to update—not decorate—the model.

Interpretation boundaries

What the integrated model does not claim.

No binding proof

Sequence similarity does not demonstrate that a PUF binds in a living cell.

No calibrated probability

The Atlas score is a prioritization heuristic, not an off-target probability.

No clinical dose

Normalized delivery parameters support comparison, not patient dosing.

No substitute for controls

Mock, inactive-editor and targeted validation experiments remain essential.
