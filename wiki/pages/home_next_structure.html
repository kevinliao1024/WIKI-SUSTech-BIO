Astrocyte

Astrocytes help manage proteins and lipids in the brain.

APOE RNA

APOE RNA encodes apolipoprotein E.

The target is the first C in CGC. Changing it to U produces UGC, corresponding to Arg112→Cys112 in mature APOE.

Astrocyte · Aβ

In human cell models, APOE4 astrocytes take up less amyloid-β than APOE3 astrocytes.

Alzheimer’s disease involves multiple interacting pathways. APOE4 influences several of them, including lipid metabolism, amyloid-β handling and inflammation.

Conceptual editing mechanism

POD recognises the RNA. CETUS edits the target C to U.

We want to know whether this RNA edit changes how astrocytes take up amyloid-β.
