Familiar places. A fading sense of home.

Inside the brain.

We follow an astrocyte, then move inside the cell to a single strand of RNA.

Meet the astrocyte.

Astrocytes support neurons and help manage proteins and lipids in the brain.

The APOE message.

APOE RNA encodes apolipoprotein E, a protein involved in transporting lipids through the brain.

One letter changes the protein.

At this site, APOE4 RNA carries C and APOE3 carries U. That single-letter difference changes one amino acid in the protein.

Amyloid can build up.

In human cell models, APOE4 astrocytes take up less amyloid-β than APOE3 astrocytes.

A higher risk of Alzheimer’s.

APOE4 increases disease risk. Carrying it does not mean someone will develop dementia.

Targeting one letter in RNA.

ORCA explores targeted C-to-U editing to shift APOE4 RNA toward an APOE3-like sequence.

From editing to evidence.

ORCA investigates whether targeted RNA editing can improve cellular function. Editing specificity, delivery and any cellular benefit remain to be tested.
