On-target RNA Correction for Alzheimer’s Disease

ORCA explores programmable on-target RNA correction strategies for Alzheimer’s disease, integrating molecular design, computational prediction and experimental validation.

Preliminary research overview. Experimental validation remains to be documented and reviewed by the team.
