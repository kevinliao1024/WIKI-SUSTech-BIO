A GUIDE TO THE PROTOCOL LIBRARY

How to read.

Start with your experimental goal, then follow the protocol IDs to the methods you need.

01

Read the protocol ID

The letters identify a method family; the two digits identify a protocol within it. For example, M01 is PCR in Molecular Biology. The number identifies the document, not an obligatory step in every experiment.

02

Choose the right level

M · C — Core methods
Individual molecular biology and cell culture techniques used across the project.
MX — Integrated workflows
Complete workflows that connect core methods, including sample handoffs and verification. Start here for mutagenesis or target-transcript RT-PCR and sequencing.
MR · RP · AS · AA · FV — Project methods
Methods for miRNA regulation, reporter construction and measurement, ASO control, AAV delivery and functional validation.
03

Find a starting point

Cloning & plasmid construction

Use M01–M07 and M13 for individual methods. MX01 connects the mutagenesis workflow; RP01 and RP02 cover reporter insert design and assembly.

RNA & expression readouts

Use M08–M11 for RNA extraction, reverse transcription and qPCR. Start with MX02 for target-transcript amplification and sequencing, or MR01–MR02 for miRNA methods.

Cells & delivery

Use C01–C04 for routine cell culture and plasmid transfection. Mimic, ASO and AAV delivery are covered by MR03, AS01 and AA01–AA04. Electroporation is in FV01.

Fluorescence & functional readouts

Use RP03–RP06 for TMP treatment and fluorescence acquisition. FV02 covers lipid imaging; FV03 covers Aβ42 exposure and supernatant ELISA.

04

Read the whole protocol

Select a numbered card to open its PDF. Check the purpose and scope first, then materials, procedure, controls, expected outcomes and troubleshooting where provided. Follow referenced protocol IDs for the connected methods, and consult the references for the original sources.

The viewer provides the version and links to open or download the complete PDF. Retain the protocol ID and version when recording which method you used.

05

Connect methods with evidence

Protocols describe how a method is performed. The Notebook records what happened in individual experiments; Measurement explains the SONAR readout. Consult the controls and interpretation limits in each PDF when using its results.
